A NOTE ON FEELS

A place to pause.

A small collection of ordinary moments that feel like something. Drawn in black and white, built with a little depth, and made for no particular purpose other than being here.

You’re seated inside each scene. Move your mouse to gently turn your head, or drag to look a little further. Use the left and right arrow keys to change feels. When the view is focused, up and down tilt your gaze and Home resets it. Choose Stay here to fill the screen; move or tap to reveal the controls, and Escape to return. Small things happen if you linger. Turn on sound if you’d like some company—headphones let you hear where things are.
